• Check off steps as you cook with a tap

• Auto shopping list with duplicate removal

• Search by ingredient or title

WHO IT'S FOR

• Food creators wanting quick reference to viral recipes
• Home cooks tired of copying TikTok recipes by hand
• Families planning weekly menus
• Anyone who loves cooking without the patience to transcribe videos
